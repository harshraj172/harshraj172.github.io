Agent failure taxonomy — annotation

What to do

There are 40 examples. For each one, open the link, read what the agent did, and find the first mistake it did not recover from. Then pick three things:

  1. Edge: which two parts of the system the mistake happened between (for example, owner ↔ model, or model ↔ tool).
  2. Fault side: which of those two parts is to blame, meaning where the fix belongs.
  3. Failure mode: the specific type of failure on that edge. The “Taxonomy” button explains each one.

Who is to blame. Blame the model if the other side did what it should have and the model could have avoided or fixed the problem. If not, blame the side that actually broke.

Please: work alone, and don't ask an AI to choose the labels. If you use one only to read a long trace, say so in the notes.

Stay away from the answers: don't open the paper on arXiv, or the data and predictions folders in the ModelOrHarness repo. They contain the labels. Open only the links on each example.

Accounts: a few examples need a free Hugging Face login, and some may ask you to sign in to Docent or X.

Not sure? If the source doesn't give enough information, tick “Insufficient evidence”, still give your best guess, and rate your confidence from 1 (low) to 5 (high).

When done: click “Export JSON” and send the file to Harsh. Your progress saves in this browser until then.
